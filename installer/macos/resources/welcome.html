Vocalyst

This installs the Vocalyst vocal plug-in and the Vocalyst Auto Key companion.

  • VST3 for Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and others
  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand
  • Standalone apps that run without a DAW

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. You can untick any part on the next pages.
